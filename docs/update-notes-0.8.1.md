# Steambirds Alliance Revive 0.8.1

Patch on 0.8.0 (27/11/2026). Client **201219.1413** and plugin **1.22.0** are unchanged. Settings and maps from 0.8.0 keep working. Later changes go to `update-notes-0.9.0.md`.

## Enemy AI editor

- Units are listed on the left: the same tree, filter and folders as the old drop-down (dungeons, biomes, generic, no origin), with Armed units only. A unit with its own AI is green. A unit with no AI file stays in the other color.
- The AI drop-down is at the top, where the unit button was. It lists the AIs of the selected unit. Picking one loads it. Load another AI, Copy id and Back to the unit's AI stay on that bar.

## Formation or AI

- On a map unit type, Formation is the first field and AI the second. Choosing one turns the other off and clears it. A formation on the type wins: the leader runs the AI saved on that formation.
- On Enemies of each biome, Formation is the column before AI. The same rule applies: one turns the other off, and a formation overrides an AI picked for that unit.

## Maps

- **Map Maker: Effects** (`maps\map_maker_effects.json`): the 55 looping client effects (`statefulEffects`), each on a still drone with its name on the floor. Shield looks come first, then the other enemy looks, the detection rings and the rest. The opening rows are status flags. `shielded` paints the unit's own art and is not a `statefulEffects` name. Generated by `tools\make_map_maker_helper.py`. Open it in the map editor and use Play test. Leave with `/leave`.

## Fixes

- A formation shield (the Sergeant Pup bubble while a pod is alive) no longer logs "shield down" every tick. The log is written only when the shield actually goes up or down.
- Place `follow` (the Pup pods, the preview and the game) flies toward a small area around the offset and may stop there. A moving point no longer throws it into a full circle. A slot stays bolted on.
- The unbound example `formations\generic\spirit_vessel.json` was removed.
